What are Quadratic Equations?

A quadratic equation is a polynomial equation of degree 2 in one variable. It has the standard form: ax² + bx + c = 0, where a ≠ 0.

✨The highest power of the variable is 2
🎯Has at most 2 real solutions (roots)
🔑Use the formula: x = (-b ± √(b²-4ac)) / 2a
🌟Discriminant (b²-4ac) tells how many solutions exist
